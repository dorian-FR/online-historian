Short presentation of the thesis

My thesis, written in French, was defended in May 2021. The thesis received a grade of 16 out of 20.

Its topic is "The Testimonies of the Members of the Health Service of the French Army on the Macedonian Front between 1915 and 1918."
To study said testimonies, I had to lay hands on written sources of people who actually witnessed the Macedonian Front: Gallipoli, Greece, and the Balkans.

Unlike soldiers who fought on the Western Front at the same time, finding testimonies of soldiers and, more specifically, people who served on the Macedonian Front and were part of the French Health Service was quite an arduous task.

With a lot of research, I was able to compile nine reliable testimonies: five doctors, two male nurses, one female nurse, and one musician-stretcher-bearer (musicien-brancardier).

Also, more than the obvious medical aspect and the difficulties inherent to the Macedonian Front, this thesis also aimed to focus on interpersonal human relations. These relations were, for example, within the French army, between the different Allied forces, between the French soldiers and their adversaries, and finally between the French soldiers and their Greek hosts, in a truly multicultural front.
